## Criterio de presentación

Se compararon [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), [Taste / redesign-existing-projects](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/redesign-skill/SKILL.md) y [frontend-design de Anthropic](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).

Se aplicaron como referencia las instrucciones de simplificación de Impeccable: eliminar mensajes genéricos, etiquetas redundantes y contenedores decorativos, conservar la identidad existente y comprobar el resultado en móvil. Taste ofrece una revisión amplia de interfaces existentes; frontend-design orienta la dirección visual, pero es menos específico para esta limpieza acotada. No se instaló ningún ejecutable ni hook.

Las [opiniones comparativas de usuarios](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeCode/comments/1syachi/best_skill_for_uxui_impeccable_vs_uxui_pro_max_vs/) son mixtas: se valora el aspecto de Taste y la consistencia de Impeccable, sin garantía de evitar resultados genéricos. La elección responde al alcance de este cambio, no a un consenso o clasificación objetiva.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial cover photograph for an economist's personal project page about machine learning and financial market forecasting. Create one wide landscape photograph, 16:9 composition. Scene: an authentic, restrained financial research desk seen from a slightly oblique overhead angle. Cream graph paper with fine unlabelled market-like line plots and a mechanical pencil in the foreground, with a portion of a matte dark laptop and its softly defocused analytical chart display in the background. Compose a simple clear focal area that works as a website card cover at small sizes. Style: high-quality documentary/editorial still-life photography, natural side light, tangible paper textures, subtle film grain. Palette to match the existing website: warm ivory paper, muted sage green, charcoal and tiny rust-orange accents. No people, no brands, no logos, no text, no legible numbers, no watermarks. This is a conceptual research illustration, not a screenshot, not actual model results, not an upward-only success chart. Avoid neon trader aesthetics, money, coins, excessive props and glossy stock-photo styling.
