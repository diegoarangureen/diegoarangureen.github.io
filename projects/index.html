Nov 2026 · Python / Rust · with my team at Junction

Thin Ice

A reinforcement-learning environment that measures how far ahead an AI model can plan. We invented a small board game, solved all 16.5 billion positions and built 12 puzzles where every move is provably right or wrong.

Across 101 runs, the models we tested plan about three moves ahead; ask for five and they usually fall through.

Nov 2026 · Python / Numba · with Xabat Arin

Time-series classifier for the GDG UAM ML Contest

Our entry for the GDG UAM ML Contest 2026: a time-series classifier (4 channels × 256 steps, 8 classes) that has to survive a train/test distribution shift and answer in well under a millisecond.

Robust cleaning, MiniRocket features and a small MLP distilled from an InceptionTime ensemble, all in one Numba call per sample: 82.5% on the demo set at 0.14 ms per sample, against the official baseline’s 43.3% at ~35 ms.

Oct 2026 · Python / LightGBM / RealMLP

MS Capital Market Forecasting

Predicting short-horizon returns from high-frequency trades and order books. My research project for Kaggle’s MS Capital financial market forecasting challenge.

Feature engineering, LightGBM baselines and RealMLP models. Changes are tested with purged time-series folds and a separate temporal confirmation step; the experiment log records what was tried and what changed.
