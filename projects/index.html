KAGGLE · QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH

MS Capital
Market Forecasting.

PythonMachine learningFinance

Finding signal in market noise.

Can we find a useful signal in the noise of financial markets? This research project tackles Kaggle’s MS Capital forecasting challenge by turning high-frequency trades and order-book activity into predictions of short-horizon returns.

Built in Python, it brings together efficient feature engineering, LightGBM and RealMLP models, and time-aware validation. The focus is as much on the research process as the prediction: testing ideas, tracking experiments and learning which signals hold up beyond the training data.
